About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the AutoCare Atlas Android application, package name com.carsaj.autocareatlas.

AutoCare Atlas is provided by CARSAJ, an automotive business based in Casablanca, Morocco. The app provides general offline vehicle-care guides, checklists, and reference information.

Data collection and sharing

AutoCare Atlas does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal or sensitive user data. All vehicle-care content is included in the installed application and does not require a network connection.

No user account
No internet permission
No advertising
No analytics
No tracking
No third-party SDKs

Information stored on your device

Saved guides, checklist progress, and appearance preferences are stored only in Android's local application preferences. CARSAJ cannot access this information.

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for this local information. Uninstalling AutoCare Atlas removes its local preferences, subject to the normal behavior of your device's operating system.

External applications

Optional Maps and Share features ask Android to open an application selected by you. AutoCare Atlas sends only the public business address or guide text visible to you. It does not receive information back from the selected application.

Any third-party application you choose to open is governed by its own terms and privacy practices.

Children's privacy

AutoCare Atlas is general automotive reference content and is not directed to children. Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

If the application's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated to explain the change and show a revised effective date. The Google Play Data safety declaration will also be updated when required.
